Privacy

Effective November 10, 2026. Operated by ASD Studios.

LolUp is in pre-release. This policy describes the current prototype and will be updated before subscriptions or public downloads begin.

The short version

LolUp reads your League of Legends data from the client on your own computer. It has no accounts, advertising or analytics. Your data only leaves your computer if you request an AI feature.

What the app reads

Using the local League client, LolUp reads your Riot ID, level, profile icon, rank and LP, champion mastery, and recent match history. Scoreboards of finished matches include the Riot IDs of the players in those matches, as they appear in the client. During champion select, it can read the visible champions and your assigned position.

LolUp does not read your password, game memory or data from a match in progress.

What is stored on your computer

LolUp saves your LP history so it can show your progress over time. It is stored only in the app's data folder:

  • Windows: %APPDATA%\app.lolup.desktop
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/app.lolup.desktop

You can delete this history at any time by deleting that folder.

What leaves your computer

Nothing is sent unless you request an AI review or plan. When you do, LolUp sends the data needed for that request to our AI provider, Anthropic, under its own terms:

  • For a review: your champion, match result and duration, your statistics, team totals and the champions in the match.
  • For a plan: your champion, your position and the visible champions.

These requests do not include your Riot ID, other players' Riot IDs or account identifiers. We do not sell your data.

This website

This website uses no cookies or analytics. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to operate the site.

Children

LolUp is not directed to children under 13.
